# Images

Save your business images in this folder. The site references them as `/images/<filename>`.

## Required

- **logo.png** — your Karvelis Global Exports logo (the one shared in chat). PNG with transparent background, 512×512 or larger recommended. Used in the Navbar, Footer, and as the OpenGraph share image.

## Recommended (replace the Unsplash placeholders)

Drop your real product photos here and update the `image:` URLs in `src/data/content.ts`:

- organic-eggs.jpg
- dehydrated-fruits.jpg
- black-sesame-seeds.jpg
- peanuts.jpg
- turmeric-fingers.jpg

Optimize images with <https://tinypng.com> before adding them — keeps the site fast on slow connections.
